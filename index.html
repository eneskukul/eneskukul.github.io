WordTail: Word Chain

Start your word with the last letter of the previous one: APPLE → ELEPHANT → TRAIN. A real-time multiplayer word game for 2–8 players, in Turkish or English rooms, with three modes (Survival, Reach the Target, King of the Hill), Quick Play, private rooms with friends and leaderboards.
